Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

TossDrive lets you move text, photos and files between your own phone and computer. All data stays in your own Google Drive. We run no server, and we never collect, see or share your data.

Data accessed

After you sign in with Google, TossDrive only accesses the "TossDrive" folder it creates in your Google Drive and what is inside it (your text messages and the photos and files you send). It cannot see any other files in your Drive. Data travels only between your browser and Google.

Data stored in your browser

A sign-in token (cleared when you close the tab), a device label, a sign-in hint email, and messages you have sent that have not finished uploading yet (so they can resume after the page is reloaded; deleted once uploaded or when you sign out), stored only in your own browser. TossDrive uses no cookies, analytics or advertising tools.

Third parties

TossDrive uses Google Sign-In and the Google Drive and Sheets services (see the Google Privacy Policy). The web page is hosted on GitHub Pages. TossDrive's use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train models.

Deleting your data and revoking access

Delete the "TossDrive" folder in Google Drive to delete all your data. To revoke access, remove TossDrive in your Google Account's third-party access settings.

Contact

Questions: GitHub Issues. If this policy changes, this page and its date will be updated.
